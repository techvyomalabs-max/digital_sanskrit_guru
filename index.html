Sanskrit books, courses and learning kits

Digital Sanskrit Guru is the online store of Vyoma Linguistic Labs Foundation, Bengaluru. We publish technology-based Sanskrit learning material for students, teachers, families and lifelong learners: grammar books, stotra and scripture collections, children's books, flipbooks, audio devices, USB sticks, web and Kindle editions, guided learning paths and value combo packs.

Physical books and devices ship across India with PIN-code based delivery, and digital editions are delivered online after purchase, most of them straight to your My Library. Bulk and gifting orders are welcome for schools, pathashalas and institutions.
